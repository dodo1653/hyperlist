How it works

01

pump.fun

The coin launches on pump.fun from your wallet, as any other: same curve, same graduation, and every trade pays you, the creator, 0.30 %. Its CA ends in hype.

02

HyperEVM twin

In the same flow, your EVM wallet deploys its twin on HyperEVM: same name and ticker, 1B supply, the pump.fun mint written into the contract, no mint or admin keys.

03

Holders claim

Holders bind a Hyperliquid address with their Solana wallet. You take one snapshot, post its root once, and each holder claims their exact balance on HyperEVM.

04

HyperCore ticker

The twin is what HIP-1 links to a ticker on Hyperliquid's order book. The price is the live 31-hour auction; your creator fees fill the meter toward it.

Straight answers. The twin is its own token on HyperEVM, claimed from a snapshot, not a live bridge: tokens bought on pump.fun after the snapshot don't follow. The HyperCore ticker takes a real HIP-1 bid in HYPE, placed by the coin's creator (for $HypeEdge, our fund wallet). Every snapshot is public and the page checks it against the root on chain. Binding is a signed message; HypeEdge never holds your funds. Every coin launched here has a CA ending in hype: the addresses are ground ahead of time on our relay, and each key signs its coin's create once, after your wallet, then pump.fun owns the mint. No fee is added to the launch.
