How it works

01

Fees

Every bonding-curve trade on pump.fun pays the coin's creator 0.30 % into a vault on Solana. We read each trade's fee straight from the program logs.

02

The meter

The vaults on one side, Hyperliquid's HIP-1 auction on the other: a 31-hour Dutch auction that falls to a 500 HYPE floor. Both read live, nothing estimated.

03

The bid

When a fund covers the price, its wallet bids and deploys the ticker (up to 6 letters) on HyperCore. Every step is posted with its transaction.

04

The twin

Genesis balances go to holders who bound a Hyperliquid address with their Solana wallet, pro rata to a snapshot of the coin.

Straight answers. The HyperCore twin is a new HIP-1 token, not a bridge of the Solana coin. Someone has to place the bid: for $HLIST it is our fund wallet, for any other coin it is that coin's creator. Binding an address is a signed message, it moves nothing, and HYPERLIST never holds your funds.
